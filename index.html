Synopsis

Saya no Uta (Song of Saya) is a renowned psychological horror visual novel written by Gen Urobuchi and produced by Nitroplus. The story centers around Fuminori Sakisaka, a young medical student whose life is derailed by a tragic traffic accident that kills his family and leaves him with severe brain damage.

As a result of an experimental surgery to save his life, Fuminori develops a severe form of agnosia. His senses are entirely distorted: the world appears as a grotesque, pulsating landscape of rotting flesh, blood, and organs. Normal people sound like screeching monsters, and normal food tastes like putrid garbage. Driven to the brink of madness and suicide by this nightmarish reality, he suddenly encounters a beautiful, pure girl named Saya. To him, she is the only normal-looking entity in the world. He clings to her for his sanity, unaware of her true nature and the dark path their relationship will take them down.

Project Description

This repository hosts the community-compiled Android APK for the visual novel Saya no Uta. This project utilizes the VNSdroid engine to provide a stable mobile experience.

How to Install

  1. Download the provided .apk file from the directory above.
  2. Ensure your Android security settings allow Install from Unknown Sources.
  3. Open the file via your device file manager to begin installation.

Compatibility

This build is tested for modern Android OS versions. If you encounter crashes upon launch, please ensure you have granted storage permissions to the application.
